# SweetGold launch kit

[English](launch-kit.md) | [简体中文](launch-kit.zh-CN.md)

## One-line description

SweetGold is a reproducible multi-agent AI lab where bee-colony policies
compete on identical worlds and models are promoted only after declared safety
and robustness gates pass.

## Short launch post

I built SweetGold to make multi-agent reinforcement-learning claims harder to
fake. Strategies run on matched seeds, training and evaluation ranges are
isolated, failed robustness audits stay visible, and promoted checkpoints are
integrity verified. The latest policy reached 100% bee survival and 0% invalid
actions across six untouched environment distributions. It is Apache-2.0 and
the rule-based Arena runs with Python alone.
